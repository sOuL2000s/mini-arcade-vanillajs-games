Self Chess

Controls & Goal:

Goal: Play both White and Black sides of a standard chess game.

Perspective: The board automatically rotates 180° after every move, ensuring the current player's side is always at the South (bottom).

Movement: Click a piece, then click a destination square to move/capture.

Note: This is a visually focused prototype. Basic move validation is implemented, but advanced rules (checkmate, castling, en passant) are omitted. The player determines the winner.

Press ESC to pause/resume the game.
