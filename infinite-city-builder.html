Minimal Metropolis

Controls & Goal:

Goal: Expand your metropolis by zoning land (R, C, I) endlessly.

Zoning: Press 1 (Residential - Blue), 2 (Commercial - Yellow), or 3 (Industrial - Red).

Build: Click an empty tile to place the selected zone type.

Score increases with every placement. Click near the bottom edge to expand the map.

Press ESC to pause/resume the game.
